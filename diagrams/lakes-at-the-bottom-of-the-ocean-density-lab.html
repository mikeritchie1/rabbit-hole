What this demonstrates

A halocline is a salinity gradient, not a solid lid. Brine can still exchange fluid, ripple and overflow. A sufficiently strong salt-density contrast tends to keep the heavier fluid down in a depression.

Limits and source material

Diagram, densities, sensor values and disturbance effects are schematic—not field measurements or a predictive fluid simulation. Real density also depends on temperature, pressure and chemical composition. Sources: NOAA Ocean Exploration, Smithsonian Ocean, and MacDonald et al. (1990).
