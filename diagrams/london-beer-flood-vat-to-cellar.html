What changes, physically?

Liquid pressure at depth depends on the height of liquid above it. The iron hoops hold the wooden staves together against outward forces. When a very large volume escapes, its mass and motion can damage other vessels and walls.

What the model cannot tell us

The condition and strengths of the original hoop and wood were never measured for us. No reliable local flood-depth map or time-resolved flow measurements survive. The failure rule, flow speed and cellar fill are illustrative—never historical predictions.
